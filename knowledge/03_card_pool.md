# Card pool: Power of Chaos: Joey the Passion only

Source: pongy's Card List v1.0 (GameFAQs #32648), 350 cards, text as in-game (2004 wording, typos original).
Scope: these 350 are the cards *introduced in this game*. The full game also accepts 421 cards from
Yugi the Destiny (155) and Kaiba the Revenge (315), 771 combined (Yugipedia says 234 new + 466 old; CrispyMallow and pongy say 350 here). The two sources
disagree on the "new" count; the 350 here are what the game's own list calls "Joey the Passion" cards.
Joey himself may also use Yugi/Kaiba cards (optional setting; always at levels 8-9).
Promo cards (Yugipedia): PCJ-EN001 Red-Eyes Black Dragon, PCJ-EN002 Sebek's Blessing, PCJ-EN003 Sword of Dragon's Soul (all in this list).

Counts: Normal monsters 73, Effect monsters 109, Fusion 9, Ritual 2, Spells 85, Traps 71 = 349 listed + #172 Lightning Conger (Normal) = 350.
Format: `#|Name|Lv Attr Type ATK/DEF|text` for monsters, `#|Name|Subtype|text` for Spells/Traps.
