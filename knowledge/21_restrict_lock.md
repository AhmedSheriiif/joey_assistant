# Thousand-Eyes Restrict lock: what is known (deck `champion_decks/Restrict_Lock.ydc`)

## From the web (Goat-format deck profile by Walter Chan, goatformat.com, 2019; other searches found no Edison-era list)
- Plan: lock the field with one or two Thousand-Eyes Restrict, absorb (equip) the opponent's monsters, and use Tsukuyomi to turn Restrict face-down again for repeated removal. The profile says the deck opens slowly and top-decks poorly, so it is weakest in the first and last turns.
- Staples around it: 3 Scapegoat, 3 Metamorphosis, Heavy Storm, 2 Waboku (they keep a Restrict from being sent to the Graveyard), 2 Magician of Faith, Morphing Jar, Sangan, Premature Burial, Snatch Steal, Mirror Force / Torrential Tribute / Call of the Haunted / Ring of Destruction.
- Not in Power of Chaos: Metamorphosis, Tsukuyomi, Breaker, D.D. Warrior Lady, Wave-Motion Cannon, Lightning Vortex, Book of Moon. Here the way to Restrict is Polymerization / Fusion Sage / Fusion Gate with Relinquished + Thousand-Eyes Idol, and Versago the Destroyer for either material.
- Sources: https://www.goatformat.com/home/deck-profile-walter-chan , https://beta.goatformat.com/deck-guides/relinquished-control

## The list (rated 87/100 by tools/rate_deck.py; the model cannot see the lock's value, so treat the number as a floor, not a promise)
Monsters 19: 2 Thousand-Eyes Idol, 3 Relinquished, 3 Versago the Destroyer, 3 Mystic Tomato, Sangan, Witch of the Black Forest, 2 Man-Eater Bug, 2 Morphing Jar, Cannon Soldier, Magician of Faith.
Spells 15: 3 Polymerization, 2 Fusion Sage, 2 Mystical Space Typhoon, Monster Reborn, Premature Burial, 2 Scapegoat, Swords of Revealing Light, Pot of Greed, Graceful Charity, Snatch Steal.
Traps 6: Call of the Haunted, Magic Cylinder, Mirror Force, Crush Card, 2 Waboku. Fusion Deck: 3 Thousand-Eyes Restrict.
Side: 3 Fusion Gate, Heavy Storm, 2 Solemn Wishes, 2 Dust Tornado, Nobleman of Crossout, Mirror Wall.
Why: 8 fusion pieces for a 2-piece fusion and 5 ways to Polymerize; Tomato, Sangan and the Witch fetch the pieces (all DARK, 1500 or less); Scapegoat and Swords buy the turns the lock needs; Reborn and Burial rebuild a destroyed Restrict.
Weak points: a destroyed Restrict (Dark Hole, Raigeki, Heavy Storm on the equipped monster), the slow start, and 2 Polymerization stuck in hand with no pieces. Not played against the computer yet.
