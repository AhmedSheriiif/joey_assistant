# Meta and strategy: what 236 tournament-level replays show

> **Correction (read first):** the figures in sections 2-4 below counted every game separately, but the replay set is made of series where one deck played up to 28 games. After removing repeats, deck size and monster count do not predict winning, and the card win-rates are weaker than shown. See `11_model_findings.md` for the corrected analysis.

Source: `My Hand` (231 replays), `My opp's Hand` (21, mostly the same games seen from the other side; 24 duplicates removed) and the 5 replays in the game's Common folder.
236 unique games, 472 decks. Full numbers in `07_learned_patterns.md` (regenerated by `python tools/ingest.py`).
Facts are labelled **[data]** (counted from replays) or **[inference]** (my reading; check before relying on it).

## 2. How the game is won
- **[data]** First player wins 56% (133/236). Median game 17 turns; 19 games end by turn 8; only 3 go past 50.
- **[data]** Final blow: direct attack in 70% of games, battle in 30%.
- **[data]** **22% of games (53/236) are finished on a turn with zero attacks**: the winner burns the opponent out. On the winning turn the most used cards are Cannon Soldier (x102), Amazon Archer (x69), Monster Reborn, United We Stand, Premature Burial, Giant Trunade, MST, Snatch Steal, Last Will.
- **[data]** Damage dealt to the loser: direct attacks 52%, battle 13%, then burn: Ring of Destruction 6%, Amazon Archer 5%, Ceasefire 4%, Cannon Soldier 4%, Magic Cylinder 3%. About a quarter of all damage is burn.
- **[data]** The loser is usually already low: median 2300 LP when the winner's last turn begins (a quarter below 1200). The win is a long chip-damage game, closed by burn/direct hits. It is not a one-turn combo game.
- **[data]** Winners make more direct attacks than losers (3.7 vs 1.4 per game) and more attacks overall (8.7 vs 6.4), and fire more effects (15.5 vs 12.4). Tempo and card use beat passivity.

- **[data]** About 19 monsters (18-21 typical), average level 3.3, under one monster of Level 5+. Everything else is spells/traps (about 21).
- **[data]** Near-universal staples (in >75% of winning lists): Sangan, Graceful Charity, Mystical Space Typhoon, Man-Eater Bug, Swords of Revealing Light, Magic Cylinder, Monster Reborn, Morphing Jar.

## 4. Turn-one behaviour (all of these are **[data]**)
- Winners play on average 0.96 monsters and 1.56 spell/traps on turn one. Losers play the same monsters and slightly more spell/traps (1.77). Overextending traps on turn one is not what wins.
- The first monster is **Set face-down 72%** of the time. Most common winning turn-one monsters: Mystic Tomato (25), Nimble Momonga (22), Man-Eater Bug (21), Giant Rat (19), Dimensional Warrior (17), Giant Germ (15), Penguin Soldier (12), Morphing Jar (10).
- Most common turn-one spell/trap: Mystical Space Typhoon (37), Scapegoat (28), Dust Tornado (25), Magic Cylinder (20), Pot of Greed (18), Ring of Destruction (17), Ceasefire (14), Waboku, Swords, Call of the Haunted.
- The modal opening (113 of 236 winners): **one Set monster + one Set spell/trap**.

## 5. Timing of key cards (median turn they are used by winners) **[data]**
Raigeki 7, Pot of Greed 8, Swords of Revealing Light 8, MST 9, Graceful Charity 10, Magic Cylinder 12, Mirror Force 12.5, Ceasefire 13, Heavy Storm 14, Torrential Tribute 14, Dark Hole 14. Pattern: draw/engine cards early, board wipes and trap payoffs mid-game (turn 12-14) once both sides have committed.

## 6. How to think (strategy rules the assistant should apply)
1. **Play the long game.** Median 17 turns. Preserve cards: set a monster, hold removal, make the opponent overcommit. **[inference from data above]**
2. **Everything revolves around chip damage plus burn.** Count the opponent's LP every turn and plan the finish: if LP ≤ roughly 2500 and you have Cannon Soldier/Archer/Ceasefire access, count the burn math before attacking.
3. **Recursive bodies are the real currency**: Giant Germ, Nimble Momonga, Mystic Tomato, Giant Rat, Dimensional Warrior, Man-Eater Bug, Sangan, Witch of the Black Forest replace themselves. Attack with them freely; their death is value.
4. **Hit directly whenever the opponent's board is empty.** Winners average 3.7 direct hits per game.
5. **Keep MST/Dust Tornado/Heavy Storm for the key trap or the lock card** (Imperial Order, Messenger of Peace, Jinzo), not the first Set card. MST is the most-played spell by winners (168 plays).
6. **Respect the three resets**: Cyber Jar/Morphing Jar flips, Torrential Tribute and Heavy Storm/Dark Hole/Raigeki. Do not commit more than you can afford to lose.
7. **Scapegoat** (160 winner plays) is both defence and Tribute/burn fuel; **Swords of Revealing Light** (172) buys three turns for the burn plan.
