# Tips: dueling Joey in Power of Chaos: Joey the Passion

Sources: CrispyMallow FAQ "Opponent Tendencies" + "Opponent Decklists" (GameFAQs #30509), Lunion guide (#39621).
**[inferred]** = my own strategic reading of the card pool, not from a source.

## A. How Joey's AI behaves (documented)
- Switches/plays monsters in Defense when their ATK is weak versus yours.
- Sets his Trap cards at the end of his last Main Phase.
- Does NOT always attack even when he has an Attack Position monster (unlike Yugi and Kaiba AIs).
- Chains Spells/Traps more intelligently than the earlier AIs. Expect face-down Quick-Play Spells.
- If he fears a Torrential Tribute, he waits until Main Phase 2 to summon.
- Can see through face-down monsters; when choosing among several, he attacks the one with the highest DEF.
- Defends with Traps in left-to-right order (your right-to-left). Knowing the order lets you time an all-out attack.
- Against flip-effect monsters he picks the "best" monster (highest ATK or DEF), furthest left, to destroy/bounce. Attack face-down monsters with your strongest attacker to avoid losing it.
- Does not chain Scapegoat when you try to remove it, even when dying.
- Does not understand that attacking Big Shield Gardna flips it into a vulnerable Attack Position, so Big Shield Gardna is a true wall against him.
- Maintenance-cost card decisions (Fairy Box, Mirror Wall, Messenger of Peace...) are complicated; don't count on him dropping them.
- Cards move along their animation paths: you can often tell what he has drawn, searched or set. Hands are shuffled after forced reveals but not on later draw phases.
- Summoning a weak monster generally baits him into attacking in Attack Position, which helps if you hold a strong card in play.
- When attacking face-up monsters with several weak ones, attack in ascending ATK order. If a Trap gets used on the weakest, the later hits go through.
- Trap that hits any summoned monster stays dangerous: Torrential Tribute triggers on any summon.

Counter-ideas **[inferred]**:
- Set #1 and #2 lean on Gravity Bind (Lv4+ cannot attack). Bring MST, Giant Trunade / Feather Duster, and Level 4-or-less attackers (Gemini Elf 1900, Axe Raider 1700, Goblin Attack Force 2300).
- Set #3 (Exodia): he can win from the deck. Use Delinquent Duo / Lightforce Sword / Ceremonial Bell (shows hand) and track his Forbidden One pieces. Banisher of the Light stops graveyard play.
- Set #4 and #6 run Fairy Box and Magic Cylinder: bait with weak attackers first (his Traps trigger left-to-right).
- Set #5 stalls with Time Seal, Fairy Box and Gardna. Kill via direct damage (Raimei, Fire Princess, Amazon Archer, Magic Cylinder) or MST his Traps first.
- Set #6 Fusion Gate: De-Fusion (Quick-Play) hits his fusions and returns material; Bite Shoes / Windstorm of Etaqua flip positions.

## C. Deck-building tips (Lunion guide)
- Run 40 cards (draws your best cards sooner). 3 copies max.
- About 15 monsters of Level 1-4 to guarantee you can always Normal Summon.
- Only about 3 monsters of Level 5+ (they need Tributes).
- Strong monsters: high ATK or high DEF, not balanced mediocre stats.
- Fill the rest with Spells and Traps.
- Include limited/forbidden cards if the ban list is off; they are good.
- Always fill the Fusion Deck (up to 15) even if you don't plan to use it. Costs nothing.
- Side deck: bring answers for Joey's deck (swap after each game in a match, since he keeps one deck for the match).
- No deck is strongest; build to beat the deck he showed you in game 1.

## D. General duel tips
- Choose first turn if you win RPS (can't attack, but you get the board up).
- Never over-extend into Torrential Tribute; keep one monster in hand if he holds 2+ Set cards.
- Leave a Spell Set in your leftmost slot if the AI destroys left-to-right (documented for Yugi's Trap Master; test against Joey).
- Don't Special Summon into Fairy Box / Magic Cylinder without a plan.
- Count his Graveyard: Premature Burial / Call of the Haunted / Monster Reborn decks win by recursion; Banisher of the Light, Soul Release, Spirit Elimination (see card pool) cut that off.
